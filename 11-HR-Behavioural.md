# Tell Me About a Challenging Production Issue

Interview Answer:

One issue involved failures in a customer onboarding workflow. Certain records were failing while others were processing successfully. I reviewed logs, compared successful and failed transactions, and identified that specific special characters were causing parsing failures. After implementing validation improvements and correcting the parsing logic, onboarding completed successfully. We also added additional validation checks to prevent similar issues in the future.

# Tell Me About a Time You Handled an Incident

Interview Answer:

During a production issue, my first priority was understanding customer impact and gathering evidence rather than making assumptions. I reviewed monitoring dashboards, analyzed logs, verified recent deployments, and worked with relevant teams to isolate the issue. Once service stability was restored, we completed a root cause analysis and implemented preventive measures to reduce the likelihood of recurrence.

---

# Describe a Time You Worked Under Pressure

Interview Answer:

Production incidents naturally create pressure because service availability and customer impact are involved. In such situations I focus on maintaining a structured approach, prioritizing communication, gathering evidence, and working through the problem methodically. Remaining calm generally leads to faster resolution than reacting impulsively.

# Questions to Ask the Interviewer

Ask at least 3-5 of these:

### About the Role

- What would success look like in the first six months?
- What are the biggest challenges currently facing the platform team?
- How is the DevOps function organized?

### About Technology

- What does your cloud landscape look like today?
- How heavily are Kubernetes and container platforms used?
- What CI/CD platform do you currently use?
- How is security integrated into your deployment process?

### About Team Culture

- How are production incidents handled?
- How are engineering ownership and accountability managed?
- What opportunities exist for technical growth and learning?

# Final HR Rule

Avoid:

```text id="bad-answer"
My previous company was bad.
My manager was bad.
The team was difficult.
```

Always use:

```text id="good-answer"
I learned a lot from the experience and am now looking for new opportunities to grow technically and contribute to larger engineering challenges.
```

This keeps the discussion professional, positive, and mature.
